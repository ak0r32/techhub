Frequently Asked Questions

What is the exam format for certifications?

Exams vary by certification level. Beginner certifications typically include multiple-choice questions and basic practical exercises. Intermediate and advanced certifications include hands-on labs, coding challenges, and real-world scenario assessments. All exams are proctored online with time limits appropriate for each certification.

How long does it take to complete a certification?

Certification durations range from 40-85 hours depending on the level. Beginner certifications typically take 4-6 weeks to complete with part-time study. Intermediate certifications require 8-10 weeks, and advanced certifications may take 12-16 weeks. You can study at your own pace, and all materials are available for one year from enrollment.

Are there any prerequisites for certifications?

Beginner certifications have no prerequisites other than basic computer literacy. Intermediate certifications typically require completion of the corresponding beginner certification or equivalent knowledge. Advanced certifications require completion of intermediate certifications or demonstrated professional experience in the field. Specific prerequisites are listed on each certification page.

How are certifications renewed?

Certifications are valid for 2-3 years depending on the level. To renew, you can either retake the current exam or complete continuing education credits through our platform. We offer renewal courses that cover updated technologies and best practices. Renewal ensures your skills remain current in these fast-evolving fields.

Is there a money-back guarantee?

Yes, we offer a 30-day money-back guarantee for all certification programs. If you're not satisfied with the course material within the first 30 days of enrollment, you can request a full refund. After 30 days, partial refunds may be available on a case-by-case basis. Exam fees are non-refundable once scheduled.
